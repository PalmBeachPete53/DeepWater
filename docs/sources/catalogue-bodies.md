# The catalogue's bodies, by country

**Evidence**: `site`/`both` = its own pages were read on 5 Nov 2026. `credited` = its site could not be read (refused, dead, script-only, none found): the subjects are those other publications credit to it, not checked at the source. **Frequency** appears only where its own pages were read, and is partly a reading of their labels.
