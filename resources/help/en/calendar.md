# Calendar

Three views, in the sub-header; the last one shown is remembered. All three are listed in [Sources](help:sources) and need no key.

## Economic

The releases of 21 large economies and the euro area: last week, yesterday, today, tomorrow, this week, next week or a date you pick, filtered by country and impact (both remembered). Each event gives the actual, the forecast and the previous value with the unit as published; the actual is green above the forecast, red below. **Surprise** is the actual minus the forecast, and **In** counts down to a release not yet out. The economic calendar gives one forecast, not the range of economists' estimates. A click on an event shows its past figures against the forecasts over three years, with a link to the official source when there is one. Times are in your time zone. The list is refreshed every ten minutes while the tab is shown. The source is TradingView's public economic calendar.

## Corporate

US listings from Nasdaq's calendars: **earnings** five weekdays back (EPS, consensus, surprise; a result without a surprise yet is dim, Nasdaq hasn't confirmed it) and ahead (time, consensus, last year), **dividends** going ex over the next seven weekdays, upcoming **splits** (reverse splits in red) and **IPOs** of last month, this month and next. Refreshed every three hours; the first load takes about half a minute.

## Market hours

21 stock exchanges and 5 futures markets, live: open, break (lunch, daily halt) or closed, the time to the next close or open, and when it happens in your time. Daylight saving is handled; a holiday is caught on the day, a quarter of an hour after the exchange should have opened. Future holidays, early closes and pre- or after-market sessions aren't known.
