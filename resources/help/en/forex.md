# Forex

The Forex page follows the seven majors (EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NZD/USD) plus EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY and USD/CNY.

## Rates

Each pair card shows the last fix, the daily change, a 90-day sparkline and the **carry** (the base currency's policy rate less the quote's). The source is the European Central Bank's reference rates: one fix per business day, no intraday prices, refreshed every six hours.

## Correlations

A matrix of the correlations of the pairs' daily log returns over 1, 3, 6 or 12 months.

## Policy rates

The central bank policy rates of the nine currencies (the BIS's daily series): the current rate, the last move, the change over a year and a three-year step chart. Euro area members get the ECB rate.

## Capital flows

The CFTC's weekly *Traders in Financial Futures* report for the CME currency futures: the net positions of asset managers and leveraged funds as a percentage of open interest, the weekly change and the 52-week history. Positions are as of Tuesday, published on Friday.

All three sources are listed in [Sources](help:sources) and need no key.
