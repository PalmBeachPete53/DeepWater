# Overview

The Overview is a snapshot of the markets, refreshed every minute while it is shown. Click a stock in the largest companies, the movers or the index movers to open it in [Research](help:research).

## Panels

- **Index performance**: SPY, QQQ, DIA and IWM as % change over today, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, year to date, 1 year, 3 or 5 years. The area from zero to the highest series is green, to the lowest red; click a name in the legend to hide a series. Overnight gaps are left out of the time axis, which is in New York time. **Statistics** beside it: per ETF, the range's return, high, low and the standard deviation of its bar returns, and the correlations of the four pair by pair.
- **Sectors**: the eleven Select Sector SPDR funds (technology, financials, health care…), today or over 1 week, 1 or 3 months or the year, sorted, with bars scaled on the largest move.
- **US indices** (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Nasdaq Composite, Dow, Russell 2000), **Global** (Nikkei, Euro Stoxx 50, DAX, FTSE, CAC, Hang Seng, TSX, IBEX, the dollar index, VIX, VXN), **Crypto** (14 coins), **FX** (12 pairs against the dollar) and **Commodities** (energy, metals, grains, sugar; grains in US cents as quoted), each in its own currency.
- **US bonds**: the Treasury's daily par yield curve from 3 months to 30 years and the change in points since the day before. It is published after the close, so it is the last business day's.
- **Largest companies**: the 30 largest US listings by market cap, ten per page, with the live price, change and market cap.
- **Gainers / Losers / Active**: today's screeners for US stocks above about $2 billion of market cap.
- **Top index gainers / losers**: today's best and worst twelve members of the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100 or the Dow.

## Sources

Yahoo Finance for the quotes and the bars (one request for every quote), Nasdaq's screener for the largest companies, the US Treasury for the yield curve. They are listed in [Sources](help:sources) under Research and Overview.
